What about us?

I am a Master student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Yohan Jo in the Human-Oriented Language Intelligence (HOLI) Lab.

I used to work on making large language models more capable and better aligned with human through training methods like preference learning, on-policy distillation, and RL. Then I blinked, and the models got really smart.

Now I spend my time wondering where that leaves us humans, and how we’re going to live together.

figuring it out, together.
