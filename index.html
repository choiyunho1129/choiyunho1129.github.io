About

01

I am a Master student in Data Science at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Yohan Jo in the Human-Oriented Language Intelligence (HOLI) Lab.

My research has primarily focused on training algorithms for large language models, including preference learning, on-policy distillation, and RL. I am now broadening this focus to human–AI interaction, studying AI and humans together as an interconnected system rather than treating models in isolation.
